A&P I — Quiz 6 & 7 Multiple-Choice Study Tool

Test-bank study tool: every question is multiple choice — there are no fill-in-the-blank items. Questions come from the real Quiz 6 (both attempts) and Quiz 7 question banks, plus extra concept drills. Two ways to run them: a round (answer all, then check) or retry mode (one question at a time — a wrong answer keeps you on that same question until you get it right, then it moves on). Anything you miss comes back with a memory hook, the trap to watch for, and the reasoning — see 🧠 Missed review, or tick Show aids while answering to see them on every question. And 🔖 Not sure — relook under any question keeps it for review even if you got it right: a coin flip that landed is not knowing it, and the score can never tell the two apart. Marked questions join the same missed pool. The 🔀 Shuffle questions box re-serves the same questions in a different order every session (and stops a 10-question session from being the same first ten forever), while 🔀 Shuffle choices moves the options too, so the answer is not always the same letter. At the end of a session, 🔁 These N again, new order reruns that exact batch reordered. It opens on the real quiz questions, because that is where the exam draws from — concept drills are still there under the question menu but they no longer come up by default — and the attempt menu narrows that further to one quiz attempt. Progress is saved in this browser.
