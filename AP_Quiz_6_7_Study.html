A&P I — Quiz 6 & 7 Multiple-Choice Study Tool

Test-bank study tool: every question is multiple choice — there are no fill-in-the-blank items. Questions come from the real Quiz 6 (both attempts) and Quiz 7 question banks, plus extra concept drills. Two ways to run them: a round (answer all, then check) or retry mode (one question at a time — a wrong answer keeps you on that same question until you get it right, then it moves on). Anything you miss comes back with a memory hook, the trap to watch for, and the reasoning — see 🧠 Missed review, or tick Show aids while answering to see them on every question. And ☐ Retry only this one under any question marks it for another pass even if you got it right — a coin flip that landed is not knowing it, and the score can never tell the two apart. Marked questions join the same missed pool, and a 🔁 Retry only these (N) button — on the home screen, in the toolbar, and at the end of every session — re-serves exactly the marked questions, reshuffled, so the same handful can be looped as many times as you like. Nothing unticks itself on a right answer; untick a question, or hit 🧽 Clear retry marks, when you are done with it. The 🔀 Shuffle questions box re-serves the same questions in a different order every session (and stops a 10-question session from being the same first ten forever), while 🔀 Shuffle choices moves the options too, so the answer is not always the same letter. At the end of a session, 🔁 These N again, new order reruns that exact batch reordered. It opens on the real quiz questions, because that is where the exam draws from — concept drills are still there under the question menu but they no longer come up by default — and the attempt menu narrows that further to one quiz attempt. 🆕 New questions first keeps you moving through the bank instead of circling it: a fresh round serves what this pool has not asked yet, so 10 at a time out of the 16-question attempt 1 is 10, then the last 6, and the same questions only come back once every one of them has been asked — the round header always says how many are still unasked. 🔄 Start this pass over resets that pass. And 📝 Retake the whole quiz — on the home screen, in the toolbar, and at the end of every session — is the one-click version of "give me the entire real quiz" for the tab you are on: all 26 of Quiz 6 (both attempts) or all 20 of Quiz 7 in a single sitting, whatever the menus and the pass currently say, with nothing drawn for you and nothing held back; Whole pool in the session-size menu now means the same thing for whichever pool the menus describe. Exam 2 is then its own tab, and it is deliberately not a third question bank: it pools all 46 real questions from both quizzes — Quiz 6's 26 (every attempt) plus Quiz 7's 20 — because the exam draws on both, so there is nothing to choose between; the attempt menu is switched off there, and Retake the whole quiz (46) runs the lot. Lecture review in the question menu serves the instructor's own Module Review questions from the Chapter 6 and 7 slides (27 for Quiz 6, 32 for Quiz 7, all 59 on Exam 2) — they are kept apart from the real quiz bank, so the quiz counts and Retake the whole quiz are unchanged. Progress is saved in this browser.
